# Smart Museum Artifact Conservation System — Task 1

| OP ID | Operations | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| OP-01 | Perform sensor and device self-check | To verify that all essential sensors and environmental-control devices are working. |
| OP-02 | Record artifact identification information | To store the identification details of the artifact. |
| OP-03 | Load artifact environmental limits | To load the required temperature and humidity limits for the artifact. |
| OP-04 | Monitor artifact and chamber conditions | To continuously monitor temperature, humidity, light, vibration, door status, and power availability. |
| OP-05 | Check chamber door status | To determine whether the chamber door is closed before active conservation. |
| OP-06 | Compare temperature with permitted range | To detect whether the temperature is outside the required limits. |
| OP-07 | Correct temperature | To restore the required temperature when it goes outside the permitted range. |
| OP-08 | Compare humidity with permitted range | To detect whether the humidity is outside the required limits. |
| OP-09 | Correct humidity | To restore the required humidity when it goes outside the permitted range. |
| OP-10 | Verify environmental recovery | To confirm through sensor readings that the environment has returned to the permitted range. |
| OP-11 | Handle unrecovered environmental conditions | To protect the artifact when an environmental condition cannot be corrected within the allowed recovery period and alert the operator. |
| OP-12 | Respond to significant vibration | To suspend risky activities and verify that vibration remains below the permitted level before normal operation resumes. |
